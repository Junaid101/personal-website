Energy Independence / 05

Strategic sovereignty starts with energy.

Decentralised, self-sufficient energy supply is at the heart of our work. It means strategic sovereignty and independence from conventional energy supplied by gas, oil or coal.

A resilient energy infrastructure can help ensure that energy can no longer be used as a strategic tool of pressure in times of crisis.
